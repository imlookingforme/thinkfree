# ThinkFree Fairness & Contestability Charter

## 1. Core Principle: Supportive, Never Surveillant or Punitive
In accordance with ThinkFree's product principles and student-first ethics, the understanding-consistency meter and any anomaly signals exist **strictly as learning-guidance advisories**. 

1. **No Automatic Punishment:**
   - No score degradation, grade reduction, account restriction, or alert to an instructor or institution may ever be triggered automatically by anomaly flags or inconsistency signals.
   - An anomalous solve pattern is treated as an indication that the student may need targeted reinforcement or an alternative validation pathway, not as evidence of misconduct.

2. **Full Mathematical & Algorithmic Explainability:**
   - Every metric (consistency score, perplexity ratio, hesitation index) is fully disclosed to the student in plain English and exact mathematical formulas.
   - The student can inspect the exact token breakdown and statistical variance that led to a low consistency score.

3. **Three-Tier Human-in-the-Loop Contestability Protocol:**
   - **Tier 1 (Instant Alternative Demonstration):** If an explanation is flagged as anomalous or inconsistent with the attempt phase, the student has an unconditional right to validate immediately via an isomorphic problem variant without penalty.
   - **Tier 2 (Self-Annotation & Clarification):** The student may submit a 1-click dispute providing context (e.g., "I solved this problem yesterday in my offline notebook", "English is my second language and I formulated my explanation using different terminology").
   - **Tier 3 (Human Review):** If contested in an institutional or course setting, the instructor is presented with the student's self-statement and attempt history alongside the advisory signal, with default presumption of academic integrity.

4. **Privacy-by-Design and DPDP Act 2023 Alignment:**
   - Keystroke events are strictly aggregated in time buckets (e.g. 5-second intervals). Raw character keylogs are never stored in analytics databases.
   - Audio recordings during explanation validation are optional and stored only with explicit student consent, retaining for a maximum of 30 days before automatic deletion.
   - Students may view, export, and request complete deletion of their telemetry data at any time.
